# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
SimpleFile a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Empathy and kindness
- Respect for differing opinions and experience
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Owning mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without permission
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They may take
appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and when someone
officially represents the project.

Report abuse privately to the maintainers. For sensitive reports you may use
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/conniecombs/SimpleFile-Linux/security/advisories/new).
All complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly.
